# Code of conduct

Be kind and assume good intent. Nori is for beginners too: no question is too basic.

- Respectful, constructive comments; critique code, not people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Keep other people's data private (tokens, ids, servers, names).

The maintainer may edit or remove comments and block people who don't follow this.
Report problems privately via the repo's **Security** tab or to the maintainer on GitHub.
